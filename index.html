🔔 Enable Notifications for StreakUp

To receive your scheduled reminders even when StreakUp is closed, please allow browser notifications:

  1. Click the 🔒 View site information (or 🎛️ Tune icon) on the left side of your browser address bar.
  2. Locate Notifications in the permissions menu.
  3. Change the setting from Blocked or Ask to Allow.
  4. Reload the page or click Enable Notifications in the header.
